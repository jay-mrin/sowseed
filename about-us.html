About Us

A personalised writing and digital book-buying platform.

Seed Garden offers personalised digital writing created from the request, prayer, intention, or message you submit. Each custom order is prepared with care and attention to the details you share, then delivered electronically.

Our Shop also offers complete digital books about faith, love, relationships, and personal growth. You may read the first five pages in the online preview before purchasing the complete book for delivery by email.

How It Works

  1. Choose a personalised writing order or select a digital book from the Shop.
  2. For a book, preview the first five pages and choose your payment amount in multiples of $7.
  3. Enter the email address where your writing or complete book should be delivered. Your name and message are optional.
  4. Complete payment securely through PayPal.
  5. We prepare the personalised writing or fulfill the book order and deliver it electronically.
