About Us

Personalised digital writing for meaningful requests.

Seed Garden creates custom digital writing from the request, prayer, intention, or message you submit. Each order is prepared as a personal piece of writing with care and attention to the details you share.

Our service is designed for customers who want a thoughtful digital message, blessing, reflection, or other personalised writing delivered electronically.

How It Works

  1. Choose the custom-order amount that suits your request.
  2. Enter your email address. Your display name and message are optional.
  3. Complete payment securely through PayPal.
  4. We prepare and deliver your personalised digital writing.
